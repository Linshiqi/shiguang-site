隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 25 日 · 开发者：Shiqi Lin

拾光是一款帮你整理照片、把照片装订成相册的 App。这份政策说明拾光会接触哪些信息、怎样使用，以及你可以怎样管理它们。

简要说明

  • 开发者不收集你的任何数据。拾光没有账户系统，也没有开发者自己的服务器。
  • 照片的识别分析和语音听写全部在你的设备上完成，照片与录音不会被上传。
  • 为了显示地名，只会把照片的大致坐标发送给 Apple 的地图服务。
  • 没有广告，没有追踪，没有第三方统计或广告 SDK。
  • 本版本不使用 iCloud 同步，相册、口述录音都只保存在这台设备上。

1我们收集的信息

没有。开发者不收集、不保存、不出售，也不与任何人分享你的个人信息。拾光不会把你的照片、录音、相册内容、使用情况或设备信息发送给开发者。下面各节说明的数据，都只保存在你的设备上。

2照片图库的访问

拾光会请求访问你的照片图库。获得授权后，照片只用于：

  • 在 App 内显示照片；
  • 在设备上识别分类、整理「那年今日」与「足迹」；
  • 在你制作的相册里排版、显示照片；
  • 读取你所在的 iCloud 共享相簿里的照片，用来制作会自动更新的家庭相册。

你可以只允许拾光访问部分照片（「有限照片访问」），也可以随时在 「设置」>「隐私与安全性」>「照片」 中更改或撤销授权。

写入照片图库：拾光只在你明确操作时改动图库：

  • 点按心形「喜欢」时，把这张照片标记为个人收藏，再次点按则取消；
  • 用相机扫描老照片后，把扫描得到的图片作为新照片存入图库；
  • 你在查看照片时选择删除，并在系统弹出的确认框中同意后，删除这张照片（照片会进入「照片」App 的「最近删除」）。

除此之外，拾光不会编辑、新增或删除你图库中的照片。相册只记录照片在图库中的标识，不复制原图；删除相册时，照片仍保留在「照片」App 中。

3在设备上进行的分析

拾光使用 Apple 的 Vision 框架，在你的设备上识别照片内容（例如人像、萌宠、美食、海边）和照片中是否有人脸，用来生成智能分类。分析过程不联网，照片不会离开你的设备。

分析结果（例如每张照片属于哪些分类，以及你手动更正过的分类）保存在 App 的本地存储中，用来让下次打开时只需分析新照片。你可以在拾光的「设置」中选择「重新整理」来清空它们。

在支持 Apple 智能的设备上，你可以让拾光为页面重新生成配文。配文由设备上的模型生成，内容不会离开设备。

4相机、麦克风与语音听写

这些权限只在你使用「老照片与口述」或在编辑页面时选择「录制口述」时才会请求，你可以随时在 「设置」>「隐私与安全性」 中关闭。

  • 相机：通过系统的文稿扫描界面拍摄老照片。扫描结果按第 2 节存入你的照片图库，拾光不保存其他副本。
  • 麦克风：录下家人对照片的讲述。录音保存在拾光在这台设备上的本地存储中，跟随对应的页面；不会上传，也不会同步到其他设备。
  • 语音听写：把讲述转为文字，作为页面的手记。听写在设备上完成：在 iOS 26 及更新版本上使用 Apple 的设备端语音模型（第一次使用时，系统可能从 Apple 下载该语言的模型，这一过程不包含你的录音或个人信息）；在更早的系统上，拾光要求系统只在设备上识别，不把语音发送到服务器。设备不支持离线听写时，拾光只录音，不做听写。

5地点与地名

如果照片带有拍摄位置，拾光会在设备上按位置把照片归到一起。为了给这些地点显示地名（例如城市名），拾光会通过 iOS 的系统服务，把照片的大致坐标发送给 Apple 的地图服务，换回地名。发送的只有坐标，不包含照片本身或其他信息。

这部分数据由 Apple 按照 Apple 隐私政策 处理。取得的地名会缓存在你的设备上，以减少重复请求。拾光不会请求或使用你当前所在的位置。

6你创建的相册

你创建的相册（标题、主题、装帧与配色、页面版式、你写的文字、口述的文字与录音、日期、地点，以及所用照片的标识）保存在你的设备上。本版本不会把这些内容同步到 iCloud。开发者无法看到这些内容。

7通知

如果你允许通知，会自动更新的相册在你不使用拾光时长出新页，拾光会在设备上生成一条本地通知告诉你。拾光没有推送服务器，通知内容不离开设备。你可以在 「设置」>「通知」>「拾光」 中关闭。

8与 Apple 服务的其他连接

  • 如果你的照片存放在「iCloud 照片」中且尚未下载到本机，系统可能会在显示照片时从 iCloud 下载，这一过程由 iOS 完成。
  • 为了显示中文衬线字体，拾光可能会请求系统从 Apple 下载一款系统字体；为了离线听写，系统可能下载语音模型（见第 4 节）。这些请求不包含你的个人信息。
  • 如果你在 iOS 中选择了「与 App 开发者共享」分析数据，Apple 可能会向开发者提供汇总的崩溃与性能报告。这项设置由你在 「设置」>「隐私与安全性」>「分析与改进」 中控制，拾光本身不收集这些信息。

9我们不做的事

  • 不展示广告，不使用任何广告 SDK；
  • 不追踪你，不读取广告标识符，不与其他公司的 App 或网站关联你的数据；
  • 不使用第三方统计、崩溃收集或任何第三方 SDK；
  • 不出售、不出租、不交换任何数据。

10删除你的数据

  • 删除相册或页面：在拾光中删除，会删除页面、文字与口述；相册先进入拾光的「最近删除」，30 天后永久删除。照片仍保留在「照片」App 中。不再被任何页面使用的录音会在下次打开拾光时清除。
  • 去掉一段口述：在编辑页面时选择「移除」。
  • 清空分析结果：在拾光的「设置」中选择「重新整理」。
  • 删除全部本地数据：从设备上删除拾光，App 保存在这台设备上的所有数据（包括录音）都会随之删除。扫描存入图库的照片属于你的照片图库，不会随 App 删除。
  • 撤销权限：在 「设置」>「隐私与安全性」 中分别更改照片、相机、麦克风与语音识别的授权。

11儿童隐私

拾光适合全家使用，常被用来整理孩子的照片、记录长辈的讲述。我们不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童（例如未满 14 周岁或未满 13 周岁的用户）的个人信息。照片中孩子的识别（「宝贝」分类）与语音听写完全在设备上完成，结果不会离开设备，也不会发送给开发者。

12数据安全

保存在设备上的数据位于 iOS 为拾光分配的独立存储空间内，受系统的数据保护机制保护。

13政策的变更

如果这份政策有变化，我们会更新本页并修改上方的生效日期。如果变化涉及拾光处理数据的方式（例如将来加入 iCloud 同步），我们还会在 App 的更新说明中写明。

14联系我们

对这份政策有任何问题，请写信至 stahere@icloud.com。

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026 · Developer: Shiqi Lin

Shiguang is an app that organizes your photos and binds them into albums. This policy explains what information Shiguang works with, how it is used, and how you can control it.

In short

  • The developer collects no data about you. Shiguang has no accounts and no servers of its own.
  • Photo analysis and speech transcription happen entirely on your device. Your photos and recordings are never uploaded.
  • To show place names, only approximate coordinates are sent to Apple's map service.
  • No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.
  • This version does not use iCloud sync. Albums and narration recordings stay on this device.

1Information we collect

None. The developer does not collect, store, sell or share your personal information. Shiguang does not send your photos, recordings, album contents, usage or device information to the developer. The data described below stays on your device.

2Access to your photo library

Shiguang asks for access to your photo library. Once you allow it, your photos are used only to:

  • show them in the app;
  • sort them into smart categories, On This Day and Places, on your device;
  • lay out and show them in the albums you make;
  • read the photos in iCloud Shared Albums you belong to, to build family albums that update themselves.

You can give Shiguang access to only some of your photos (limited access), and you can change or revoke access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

Writing to your library. Shiguang changes your library only when you ask it to:

  • when you tap the heart, it marks that photo as a Favorite, and tapping again removes it;
  • when you scan old photos with the camera, it saves the scans to your library as new photos;
  • when you delete a photo while viewing it and confirm in the system prompt, it deletes that photo (it moves to Recently Deleted in the Photos app).

Apart from that, Shiguang does not edit, add or delete photos in your library. Albums store an identifier for each photo, not a copy of it, and deleting an album leaves your photos in the Photos app.

3Analysis on your device

Shiguang uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to recognize what is in a photo (for example people, pets, food or the seaside) and whether it contains faces, to build smart categories. This analysis does not use the network, and your photos do not leave your device.

The results (which categories a photo belongs to, including categories you correct by hand) are kept in the app's local storage so that later only new photos need to be analyzed. You can clear them with Reorganize in Shiguang's Settings.

On devices that support Apple Intelligence, you can have Shiguang regenerate a page's caption. Captions are generated by the on-device model, and the text does not leave your device.

4Camera, microphone and speech transcription

These permissions are requested only when you use Old Photos & Narration or choose Record Narration while editing a page. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security.

  • Camera: used through the system's document scanner to capture old prints. Scans are saved to your photo library as described in section 2; Shiguang keeps no other copy.
  • Microphone: records a family member telling the story of a photo. Recordings are stored in Shiguang's local storage on this device, with the page they belong to. They are never uploaded and are not synced to other devices.
  • Speech transcription: turns the narration into the page's note, on the device. On iOS 26 and later, Shiguang uses Apple's on-device speech model (the first time, iOS may download the model for your language from Apple; that download contains no recording or personal information). On earlier versions, Shiguang requires on-device recognition so speech is never sent to a server. If your device can't transcribe offline, Shiguang records without transcribing.

5Locations and place names

If a photo has a location, Shiguang groups photos by place on your device. To name those places (a city, for example), Shiguang uses an iOS system service to send the photo's approximate coordinates to Apple's map service and receive a place name. Only the coordinates are sent, never the photo or anything else about it.

Apple handles this data under the Apple Privacy Policy. Place names are cached on your device to avoid repeat requests. Shiguang does not ask for or use your current location.

6Albums you create

The albums you make (titles, themes, bindings and colors, page layouts, the words you write, narration text and recordings, dates, places, and identifiers for the photos used) are stored on your device. This version does not sync them to iCloud. The developer cannot see them.

7Notifications

If you allow notifications, Shiguang creates a local notification on your device when an album that updates itself gains new pages while you're not using the app. Shiguang has no push server, and the notification never leaves your device. You can turn notifications off in Settings > Notifications > Shiguang.

8Other connections to Apple services

  • If a photo is stored in iCloud Photos and not yet on your device, iOS may download it from iCloud when it is shown.
  • To show Chinese text in a serif typeface, Shiguang may ask iOS to download a system font from Apple, and for offline transcription iOS may download a speech model (see section 4). These requests contain no personal information.
  • If you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS, Apple may give the developer aggregated crash and performance reports. You control this in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements; Shiguang itself does not collect this information.

9What we don't do

  • No ads, and no advertising SDKs;
  • No tracking, no reading of the advertising identifier, and no linking your data with other companies' apps or websites;
  • No third-party analytics, crash reporting or other third-party SDKs;
  • No selling, renting or trading of any data.

10Deleting your data

  • Delete an album or a page: deleting it in Shiguang removes its pages, text and narration. Albums first go to Shiguang's Recently Deleted and are removed permanently after 30 days. Your photos stay in the Photos app. Recordings no page uses any more are cleared the next time you open Shiguang.
  • Remove a narration: choose Remove while editing the page.
  • Clear analysis results: choose Reorganize in Shiguang's Settings.
  • Delete everything on a device: delete Shiguang from the device, and all data the app stored there, recordings included, is removed with it. Photos you scanned into your library belong to your library and stay there.
  • Revoke permissions: change Photos, Camera, Microphone and Speech Recognition access in Settings > Privacy & Security.

11Children's privacy

Shiguang is made for the whole family and is often used for photos of children and the stories of grandparents. We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children (for example users under 13, or under 14 in mainland China). Recognizing children in photos (the Kids category) and transcribing speech happen entirely on the device. The results never leave it and are never sent to the developer.

12Security

Data on your device lives in the storage iOS sets aside for Shiguang and is protected by the system's data protection.

13Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change affects how Shiguang handles data (for example, adding iCloud sync in the future), we will also say so in the app's release notes.

14Contact

If you have questions about this policy, write to stahere@icloud.com.
