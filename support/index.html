常见问题

我的照片会被上传吗？

不会。照片的识别分析和语音听写都在设备上完成，拾光没有服务器，也不收集任何数据。为了显示地名，只会把照片的大致坐标发送给 Apple 的地图服务。详见隐私政策。

为什么看不到我的照片？

拾光需要访问照片图库。请在「设置」>「隐私与安全性」>「照片」>「拾光」中选择「完全访问」或选取照片。没有授权时，仍可以翻阅内置的示例相册。

相册会占用很多空间吗？

不会。相册只记录照片在图库中的位置，不复制原图。删除相册时，照片仍保留在「照片」App 中。

怎样让相册自动添加新照片？

从智能分类、足迹、「那年今日」或 iCloud 共享相簿创建相册时，打开「自动添加新照片」。之后拍摄（或家人添加到共享相簿）的照片会自动添加为新页。

老照片与口述需要什么？

扫描需要相机，口述需要麦克风。在支持离线听写的设备上，讲述会在本机转为文字；不支持时只保存录音，可以手动输入文字。录音只保存在这台设备上。

怎样给长辈用大字翻阅？

翻开任意一本相册，点右上角的「更多」，选择「大字翻阅」。轻点左右两侧翻页，点左上角的叉回到标准翻阅。

怎样导出或冲印？

在相册里点「更多」>「导出」，可以选择视频或 PDF（6 × 9 英寸，可直接冲印成照片书）。

删除的相册能找回吗？

可以。删除的相册会在书架底部的「最近删除」里保留 30 天。

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Photo analysis and speech transcription happen on your device. Shiguang has no servers and collects no data. To show place names, only approximate photo locations are sent to Apple’s map service. See the privacy policy.

Why can’t I see my photos?

Shiguang needs access to your photo library. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Shiguang and choose Full Access or select photos. Without access you can still read the bundled sample album.

Do albums take up a lot of space?

No. Albums point to photos in your library instead of copying them. Deleting an album leaves your photos in the Photos app.

How do albums add new photos automatically?

When you create an album from a smart category, a place, On This Day or an iCloud Shared Album, turn on Add New Photos Automatically. Photos you take later (or that family adds to the shared album) become new pages.

What does Old Photos & Narration need?

Scanning uses the camera and narration uses the microphone. On devices that support on-device transcription, the story becomes text on the device; otherwise the recording is kept and you can type the text. Recordings stay on this device.

How do I set up Large Print for a grandparent?

Open any album, tap More in the top corner and choose Large Print. Tap either side to turn the page; tap the X to return to standard reading.

How do I export or print an album?

In an album, tap More > Export and choose Video or PDF (6 × 9 inches, ready to print as a photo book).

Can I get a deleted album back?

Yes. Deleted albums stay in Recently Deleted at the bottom of the shelf for 30 days.
